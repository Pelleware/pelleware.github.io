# Walkabout Support

Walkabout turns every street you walk green, until you've walked your whole city.

**Contact:** [pelleware@gmail.com](mailto:pelleware@gmail.com). Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## Common questions

**Does Walkabout track me all the time?**
No. Your route is only recorded during a walk you start. Nothing runs in the background otherwise.

**A street I walked didn't turn green.**
Keep Precise Location turned on for Walkabout (Settings › Walkabout › Location). Without it, Walkabout can't tell which street you're on. Either side of the street counts. A short side street counts once you've walked into it; GPS gaps (for example in tunnels) aren't filled in.

**Recording stopped by itself.**
Walkabout stops recording when it detects cycling or travel in a vehicle. Walks only count on foot.

**Can I leave out areas I won't walk?**
Yes. In Settings, choose the areas that count. Your percentage only counts the areas you keep.

**How do I keep my walks safe?**
Your walks are stored only on your iPhone. Save a backup from History to Files or iCloud Drive now and then. If you delete Walkabout or move to a new iPhone, you can restore it from that file in Settings › Restore backup.

**What happens if I force-quit Walkabout during a walk?**
The walk stops. The part you recorded is saved, and Walkabout tells you about it the next time you open it. To keep recording, leave Walkabout running in the background; locking the screen is fine.

**Can I walk every street on the map?**
The map shows public streets and paths, but access information isn't always complete. Always follow signs on the ground, and skip anything that's private or unsafe. Completion Assist in Settings can finish an area when the last streets can't be reached.

**Do I need an account or internet?**
No account. The map background comes from Apple Maps and needs a connection to load; your walks are recorded either way.

**Which cities are available?**
You can pick your city in Settings. More cities are on the way.

## Privacy

Walkabout collects no data about you. Read the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
