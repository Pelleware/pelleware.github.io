# Walkabout Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 17 October 2026*

Walkabout is made to keep your walks to yourself. In short: **we don't collect any data about you.** There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything Walkabout records stays on your iPhone.

## What Walkabout records, and where it stays

**Your location, only during a walk.** Walkabout uses your location to centre the map, and records your route only while a walk you started is running, including while the screen is locked. It doesn't track you in the background or when no walk is running.

**Your walks and progress.** Routes, distances, the streets you've covered, your weekly goal and your settings are stored on your iPhone in Walkabout's own storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

**Motion activity, only during a walk.** Walkabout uses your iPhone's motion data to stop recording when it detects cycling or travel in a vehicle. This data isn't stored.

## Things that only happen when you choose them

- **Apple Health.** If you allow it, Walkabout saves each finished walk to Apple Health as a walking workout with its route. Walkabout doesn't read anything from Health. You can turn this off in Walkabout's Settings or in the Health app.
- **Photos.** Walkabout adds a picture of a walk to your photo library only when you choose to save it while sharing.
- **Sharing.** When you share a walk, a picture or a file, it goes only to the app or person you choose.
- **Backups.** When you save a backup, the file goes where you put it (for example Files or iCloud Drive). It contains your walks and routes, so keep it somewhere private.
- **Notifications.** Reminders during a walk and about your weekly goal are created on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Apple services

The map background is provided by Apple Maps. To show it, your iPhone asks Apple for the map area you're looking at, as any app with a map does. That is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Walkabout's widgets, Live Activity and Siri shortcuts run on your iPhone and use the same local data.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Walkabout from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Walks saved to Apple Health and backup files you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them there.

## Children

Walkabout doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
