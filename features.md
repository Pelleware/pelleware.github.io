# Everything Walkabout can do

Walkabout turns every street you walk green, until you've walked your whole city. Here's everything in the app.

## Walking

**Start a walk, then just walk.** Tap Start walk and put your phone in your pocket. Your route is recorded until you tap Finish walk, also with the screen locked.

**Either side of the street counts.** Walk on whichever pavement you like; the street turns green either way. A side street counts once you've walked into it, and turning back just before the end of a street still counts the whole street.

**Only real walking counts.** Walkabout stops recording if it detects cycling or a ride in a vehicle, and keeps the part you walked. GPS gaps, for example in tunnels, are never filled in, and when the GPS signal is weak, streets it isn't sure about wait until it is.

**Check-ins on long walks.** If you haven't moved for 5 minutes, or have been recording for an hour, Walkabout checks that you still want it to keep going.

**Warnings at the edge of the map.** Walks can start up to 2 km outside the city. If you like, Walkabout warns you when you're about to walk off the map, where streets stop counting.

**End walks on purpose.** Turn on Confirm before ending a walk so a stray tap never ends one.

## Your map

**Watch your city turn green.** The map shows every street you've walked in green, with your route of the current walk drawn as you go.

**See what's left.** Show the remaining streets to see exactly which ones you haven't walked yet.

**Public streets and paths only.** The map shows the streets and paths you can walk, one line per street, built from OpenStreetMap. Always follow signs on the ground and skip anything private or unsafe.

## Areas and progress

**Your city in areas.** Each city is split into areas, like Copenhagen's districts. Coverage shows how much of the city and of each area you've walked, and which walks explored an area.

**Pick the areas that count.** Leave out the areas you'll never walk. Your percentage, milestones, widgets and Siri then count only the areas you keep. Walks elsewhere are still recorded, and switching an area back on brings its progress back.

**Completion Assist.** Some streets just can't be walked. Once an area is 95 % complete, Completion Assist can finish it for you. It's off until you turn it on.

**Reset progress.** Walked an area to 100 %? Reset it and walk it again. Walkabout counts how many times you've completed each area. Your walks, history and stats are always kept.

## Goals and celebrations

**Weekly goal.** Set a goal for new ground, distance or number of walks each week. Reach it week after week to build a streak.

**A reminder on the last day.** If you like, Walkabout reminds you on Sunday afternoon, only when you haven't reached your goal yet.

**Milestones.** Your walk summary marks the moment you pass 25 %, 50 % and 75 % of your city or your area.

**Celebrations.** Completing an area, or your whole city, gets a celebration that traces every street you walked. You can watch it again whenever you like.

## History and stats

**Every walk, saved.** History lists your walks with their route, distance, time and new ground, and the areas each one explored. Give a walk a name, or delete one you don't want.

**Replay a walk.** Watch a walk play back on the map as the streets turn green.

**Your stats and records.** See this week, this month and all time, plus your records: longest walk, longest time on a walk, most new ground, best week and longest streak.

## Sharing

**Share a walk.** Share a picture of your walk's route and new ground, or switch the picture to your whole city (or your area) in green with your progress. Light or dark, with a message to go with it.

**Export GPX.** Save a walk's route as a GPX file for apps like Strava or Komoot.

**Apple Health.** Walkabout can save your walks to Apple Health as walking workouts. It never reads anything from Health.

## Lock Screen, widgets and Control Center

**Live Activity.** During a walk, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show your time, distance and new ground, updating as you go.

**Home Screen widgets.** *Start walk* shows your recent new ground and starts a walk with one tap. *This week* shows your week of walks; tap a day to see it.

**Lock Screen widgets.** Your city progress, your weekly goal, and this week's new ground at a glance.

**Start a walk from Control Center.** Add the Start walk control to Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.

## Siri and Shortcuts

**Ask Siri.** "Start a walk in Walkabout", "End my walk in Walkabout" or "How much have I walked in Walkabout?" The same actions are in the Shortcuts app.

## Privacy and your data

**No tracking outside walks.** Walkabout only uses your location between Start walk and Finish walk. Nothing runs in the background otherwise.

**No account, no server.** There's nothing to sign up for. Your walks stay on your iPhone, and Walkabout collects no data about you. Read the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

**Backups.** Save a backup of your walks to Files or iCloud Drive, and restore it on a new iPhone. A restore can be undone, and Walkabout reminds you when your newest walks aren't backed up yet.

## Cities, languages and settings

**Four cities.** Copenhagen and Frederiksberg, Aarhus, Odense and Manhattan. Each city keeps its own progress, so you can switch and come back.

**Map updates keep your progress.** When a city's map is improved, your walks are replayed onto the new map automatically.

**English and Danish.** Walkabout follows your iPhone's language, or pick one in Settings.

**Make it yours.** Light, dark or automatic appearance, metric or imperial units, and haptics on or off.
