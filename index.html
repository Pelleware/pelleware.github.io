Walk every street in your city.

Walkabout is an iPhone app that turns the streets you walk green. Start a walk, go wherever you like, and watch your city fill in, one street at a time.

Coming to the App Store.

How it works

Walk. Tap Start walk and put your phone in your pocket. Either side of the street counts, and your route is only recorded while a walk is running.

Fill in your areas. Your city is split into areas. Walk every street in one to complete it, then move on to the next. Leave out the areas you'll never walk, and your percentage only counts the rest.

Keep it yours. There's no account and no server. Your walks stay on your iPhone, and you can back them up to Files whenever you like.
