# Walkabout

Every street you walk turns green, until you've walked your whole city.

Walkabout is an iPhone app for exploring your city on foot. Start a walk and go: the streets you cover fill in on the map, area by area. No account, no tracking outside your walks, and your walks stay on your iPhone.
